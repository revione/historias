# Rhetoric - the tone that opens

## What I want to study

There's a guy whose way of talking with women I want to analyze. His videos always end well — he doesn't say anything out of the ordinary, but his tone makes them respond by surrendering to the situation: without judgment, with innocence, but with reality and playfulness.

## What I want to practice

**Rhetoric with that tone** — understanding how he builds that atmosphere with voice and words. What exactly he does to make the climate what it is.

**Paraphrasing** — taking what the person said and giving it back as if it were an experience, with different words. Not repeating: reformulating in a way the other person feels as something alive. I'm still very raw at that.
