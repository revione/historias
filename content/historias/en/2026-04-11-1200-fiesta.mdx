# Party - the girl in line

## The entrance

The energy I brought to the door was so off that the bouncer wanted to card me. I showed him photos of my ID and he let me in, though it was pretty uncomfortable.

## The girl in line

A beautiful girl asked me if they could get in with the ticket they had because there were 5 minutes left. I said yes.

That same girl pressed against me when checking in the jacket. I got the sense she was floating. Later on the dance floor, she showed me several times how she moved, hinting at closeness.

## What happened (or didn't happen)

Since I had been smoking, I didn't feel in my natural state for approaching. The signals were there, I wasn't.
