# Part I — Amy Eskridge: Complete Research Agenda

Amy Eskridge was an American **chemist and entrepreneur** with an unusual profile: she combined scientific training with a deliberate effort to engage in **public outreach** in areas generally confined to classified programs or small university departments. Her documented output centers on **presentations, talks, and corporate filings**, not an extensive peer-reviewed bibliography.

## 1.1 Corporate Structure She Led

| Entity | Amy's Role | Nature | Current Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Institute for Exotic Science** (P.B.C.) | President and co-founder | Public Benefit Corporation registered in Delaware, based in Huntsville | Website inaccessible after her death (June 2022) |
| **HoloChron LLC** | CEO, co-founder | Father-daughter family company | Dissolved December 29, 2022 |

Her father **Richard Eskridge** (retired NASA Marshall engineer, specialized in plasma and fusion) was listed as CTO and co-founder.

## 1.2 The December 2018 HAL5 Conference

On December 6, 2018, Eskridge gave the talk **"A Historical Perspective on Anti-Gravity Technology"** at the **Huntsville Alabama L5 Society (HAL5)** in the main auditorium of the Madison County public library. The complete PDF of the presentation is publicly accessible. This is the complete structure of her overview:

### Physical Fundamentals She Covered

- **Definitions:** distinction between gravity (fundamental force, 10⁻³⁹ times weaker than the strong force, infinite range) and anti-gravity (reduction, cancellation, or shielding from gravity).
- **Historical milestones:** Copernicus (1543), Kepler (early 1600s), Newton (Universal Law of Gravitation, 1686), Cavendish (first measurement, 1790s), Einstein (general relativity, 1916).
- **Modern framework:** Standard Model of particles, the four forces (strong, weak, electromagnetic, gravitational), the search for the **graviton** (postulated in 1934 by Blokhintsev and Gal'perin), and the open question of whether gravity is quantized.

### Historical Research She Covered

| Year | Researcher | Concept | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1920s | Thomas Townsend Brown | **Gravitator / Biefeld-Brown effect / electrogravitics** | Patent 1960; vacuum tests "supposedly rule out ion wind"; no modern support |
| 1928 | Nikola Tesla | **Space Drive / Patent 1,655,144** — anti-electromagnetic propulsion | Tesla rejected spacetime curvature and proposed "ether vortices" |
| 1948 | Roger W. Babson | **Gravity Research Foundation** | Annual prize won by Hawking and 5 Nobel laureates |

| 1974 | Eric Laithwaite | **Gyroscopes** | Maglev inventor; claimed rotating disc generates orthogonal force; explicable by Newton's 2nd law |

### The Pivot Point: Mansfield Amendment (1973)

Eskridge devoted a critical section to this amendment. It **restricted Defense Department funding to research with direct military application**, which in practice pushed anti-gravity research out of the open academic sphere toward:

- The **private sector**.
- **Black budgets**.

Her thesis: this displacement is the reason why it is today difficult to follow the open trail of serious research in the field.

### Modern Research Lines She Detailed

| Year | Research Line | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1970s | **Hutchison Effect** | "Anomalous effects" attempting to recreate Tesla's experiments; no reproducibility |
| 1990 | **Woodward Effect / Mach Effect** | Reactionless propulsion proposed by Jim Woodward; theorized to enable Warp Drive (Alcubierre Drive) |
| 1990s | **Ning Li & Doug Torr / AC Gravity** | Magnetic field on superconductor aligns ionic spins → gravitomagnetic/gravitoelectric effect. UAH-NASA Marshall collaboration ~1996. **DoD contract of US$500,000 in 2001 to AC Gravity LLC** |
| 1990s–2000s | **Podkletnov Effect** | Rotating YBCO disc produces "gravitational shielding" of 2%; patent 2001; collaboration with NASA Marshall in the 2000s |

| 2000s–present | **Morningstar Applied Physics** (Paul Murad and John Brandenburg) | Modified Searl Machine with GEM theory (Gravito-Electric-Magnetism); reported 7% weight reduction |
| 2010s | **Boeing Phantom Works GRASP** | "Gravity Research for Advanced Space Propulsion" program publicly acknowledged in 2014; Podkletnov reported as collaborator |

### The "Academic Disappearances" She Documented

In her talk she identified a pattern she called _"panning"_:

- **Ning Li:** The Popular Mechanics article covering her was no longer accessible at the time of the talk.
- **Doug Torr:** His research at the University of South Carolina was never published publicly.
- **Eugene Podkletnov:** Fired from the University of Tampere in 1997.
- **Giovanni Modanese:** Wikipedia page deleted.

### Her Conclusion

Eskridge closed the talk with three proposals:

1. **Need for theories with testable hypotheses.**
2. **Independently funded research by private interests.**
3. The open question: _"How do we fix the fact that promising results always disappear into the classified domain?"_

## 1.4 The Blocked Research of 2020

In 2020, according to interviews she herself gave:

- Announced **"foundational research" on anti-gravity** that required **NASA approval** before she could present it publicly.
- NASA promised to respond within 30 days.
- The approval never came.
- Her public statement: _"I have to publish because it is only going to get worse until I publish."_

There is no public record of what that research specifically consisted of.

## 1.5 Peer-Reviewed Production

It is important to state this honestly: **Eskridge does not have a substantive peer-reviewed bibliography in the field**. Her documented track record consists of:

- Public presentations (talks, lectures).
- Corporate filings (Delaware, Alabama).
- Didactic outreach material.
- Podcast appearances (Jeremy Rys, 2020).

This places her in an intermediate zone between formal academic research and the entrepreneurial/activist domain of science communication.

# Part II — What Each Person Was Working on at the Time of the Incident

This is the question most often lost in general coverage. Here is each case with the most specific detail that could be reconstructed from primary and institutional sources.

## 2.1 Ning Li — struck by a vehicle 2014, died 2021

**Work status at the time of her accident (2014):**

- Had left UAH in 1999 to found **AC Gravity LLC**.
- In 2001 obtained a **Department of Defense contract for US$500,000** to continue her research.
- After 2003, "disappeared" from the public record (not from life — it was a personal/corporate decision).
- In 2014 she was struck by a vehicle on the university campus; she suffered severe brain damage.
- Died in 2021 from Alzheimer's, a secondary consequence of the trauma.

Her last active research agenda was **scaling her theoretical models of gravitational shielding/modification via superconductors**, in intermittent collaboration with NASA Marshall, until the effective end of her career in 2014.

## 2.2 Amy Eskridge — died 2022

**Work status at the time of her death (June 2022):**

- R&D Director of the Institute for Exotic Science and CEO of HoloChron LLC.
- Working on what she described as **"new foundational research on anti-gravity"** that she had hoped to present publicly since 2020 (blocked by pending NASA approval).
- A few weeks before her death, her Institute had scheduled internal conferences on **Technology Demonstrator Mission for Fast Solar Wind Sailing** (planned for July 2022) and **Direct Fusion Drive for Space Power and Propulsion** (planned for August 2022).
- HoloChron LLC was formally dissolved six months after her death.

## 2.3 Michael David Hicks — died 2023

**Work status at the time of his death (July 2023):**

- Had concluded his active time at NASA JPL in **2022**, after 24 years of service.
- At the time of death was in a **closing/legacy phase**, not in an active project.
- His previous science teams continued operating: **DART** (impact in September 2022, data being analyzed), **Dawn**, **NEAT**, **Deep Space 1**.
- Had just published several articles on NEO photometry.
- His daughter Julia Hicks has publicly stated that he had **pre-existing medical conditions**.
- The official cause of death according to the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner was **arteriosclerotic cardiovascular disease**.

## 2.4 Matthew James Sullivan — died 2024

**Work status at the time of his death (May 2024):**

- Retired USAF intelligence officer. Working for a **private corporation collaborating with an American intelligence agency** (not publicly identified).
- According to Rep. Eric Burlison and reports in Liberation Times, was directly involved in the **technical/technology aspects** of an alleged **"legacy UFO program"** — a supposed program for recovering unidentified objects.
- Had a **scheduled appearance before Congress in November 2024** as a whistleblower under coordination with David Grusch.
- Died 6 months before the scheduled date of testimony.

> Official cause of death (Medical Examiner of Northern Virginia District): accidental overdose from a lethal mixture of alcohol, alprazolam, cyclobenzaprine, and imipramine.

## 2.5 Frank Maiwald — died 2024

**Work status at the time of his death (July 2024):**

Maiwald was in **full technical production**, not close to retirement. He was **JPL Principal** (internal designation granted only to scientists of exceptional contribution).

**Active projects:**

1. **SBG-VSWIR (Surface Biology and Geology Visible to Shortwave Infrared)** — _managing instrument development_. It is a hyperspectral imaging spectrometer from **360 to 2,500 nm with 10 nm contiguous spectral sampling and 30 m spatial sampling, with a 185 km cross-track swath**. It is part of the **NASA Earth System Observatory (ESO)**, recommended by the 2017 Decadal Survey "Thriving on Our Changing Planet" as Designated Targeted Observable. The RFI for the spacecraft bus was issued in September 2021.
2. **AMR-C (Advanced Microwave Radiometer for Climate)** — had concluded instrument delivery.
3. **Previous active contributions:** AMR/SWOT (Surface Water and Ocean Topography), COWVR (Compact Ocean Wind Vector Radiometer), AMR/Jason 3, **HIFI (Heterodyne Instrument for the Far Infrared)** of the Herschel Observatory.
4. Programs associated with **detection of signs of life on icy moons (Europa, Enceladus)**.

His technical specialty: **microwave radiometry** and **terahertz spectroscopy**. His instruments have civilian applications (climate, agriculture, glaciology) but also **dual-use** applications (maritime domain surveillance, target tracking in any weather).

## 2.6 Anthony "Tony" Chavez — disappeared 2025

**Work status at the time of his disappearance (May 2025):**

- Was **already retired since 2017**.
- Spent his career as a **foreman supervising construction** at LANL — specifically, according to the New York Post, much of that time at the **Dual-Axis Radiographic Hydrodynamic Test Facility (DARHT)**, located in Technical Area 15, Area III of Los Alamos.
- DARHT is a critical facility of the Stockpile Stewardship Program (DOE/NNSA): **two linear induction accelerators generate electron pulses (60 ns, 2 kA, 20 MeV) that produce X-ray radiographs of hydrotests**, simulations at scale of the processes that detonate nuclear weapons.
- In 2022 DARHT had completed its **75th hydrodynamic test** and was in the process of modernization (DCX — DARHT Capability eXpansion Strategy).
- His specific role: overseeing the construction of the physical facilities, not the scientific measurements.

## 2.7 Monica Jacinto Reza — disappeared 2025

**Work status at the time of her disappearance (June 2025):**

- Position: **Director of the Materials Processing Group at NASA JPL**.
- Previous career: **37+ years at Aerojet Rocketdyne** as Technical Fellow for Materials and Processes Engineering (the company's highest technical category).
- **Associate Fellow of AIAA**.

**Active projects:**

1. **Mondaloy** — the nickel superalloy family she co-invented. Patent **US 2010/0266442 A1, "Burn-Resistant and High Tensile Strength Metal Alloys"**, granted October 21, 2010 (with Jacinto et al. as inventors). Continued to be the material of choice for:
   - The **Aerojet Rocketdyne AR1 engine** — the American response to replace the Russian **RD-180** engines in national security launches.
   - The **Hydrocarbon Boost Technology Demonstrator**.
2. **Cost-sharing program with AFRL** since 1999 to scale production and refine Mondaloy processing methods. The connection with McCasland originates here: **he was Materiel Wing Director of the AFRL Space Vehicles Directorate and commander of the Phillips Research Site at Kirtland AFB (2001–2004)**, exactly the period when the AFRL Materials Directorate funded the government side of the Mondaloy project.
3. Her mentor Hardwick subsequently moved to AFRL at Wright-Patterson AFB. By 2005, Reza led all materials research for advanced gas turbine engines.

Reza was not an assistant — she was one of the **most important materials scientists in American aerospace**, with three patents to her name, trained at Columbia (B.S. Metallurgy) and UCLA (M.S. Materials, 1997).

## 2.8 Melissa Casias — disappeared 2025

**Work status at the time of her disappearance (June 2025):**

- Administrative assistant at LANL. Her niece Jazmin McMillen (who has reviewed police documents) has confirmed that she **did not have high-level clearance or access to technical classified information**.
- On the day of her disappearance, she had forgotten her work badge and stayed working from home.

No specific scientific project is associated with her role. Her work was administrative support.

## 2.9 Joshua Kyle LeBlanc — died 2025

**Work status at the time of his death (July 2025):**

LeBlanc was an aerospace electrical engineer at **NASA Marshall Space Flight Center**, Huntsville, Alabama (the same city as Amy Eskridge and her father Richard).

**Active projects:**

1. **NASA Space Nuclear Propulsion (SNP) Instrumentation and Control maturation** — was **team lead** in the maturation of instrumentation and control for space nuclear propulsion systems.
2. **DRACO (Demonstration Rocket for Agile Cislunar Operations)** — joint DARPA-NASA program with Lockheed Martin (vehicle) and BWXT (HALEU reactor).
   - Goal: **first in-orbit demonstration of a nuclear thermal rocket engine**.
   - Similar thrust to chemical propulsion, but **2 to 5 times more efficient** (higher specific impulse).
   - Fuel: **high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU)** that heated liquid hydrogen to high temperatures.

> ⚠️ **Critical chronological point:** DRACO was **cancelled by DARPA in May–June 2025**, weeks before LeBlanc's death in July. The official reason: the FY2026 President's Budget Request allocated no funds for nuclear thermal or electric propulsion. DARPA transferred the knowledge to NASA. At the time of LeBlanc's death, his program was in the process of **closure and transition**.

## 2.10 Steven Garcia — disappeared 2025

**Work status at the time of his disappearance (August 2025):**

- Government security contractor for the **Kansas City National Security Campus (KCNSC)** in Albuquerque, New Mexico.
- KCNSC is operated by **Honeywell FM&T** under the supervision of the **National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA)**.
- The facility produces **more than 80% of the non-nuclear components** of U.S. nuclear weapons.

**Specific role:** **Property Custodian** with **top security clearance**. According to anonymous sources cited by the Daily Mail, his position was _"very high level, overseeing all assets. Tens, perhaps hundreds of millions of dollars in equipment and assets, some unclassified, others classified."_

KCNSC works closely with **Sandia National Laboratories** and **Los Alamos National Laboratory**.

## 2.11 Nuno Loureiro — died 2025

**Work status at the time of his death (December 2025):**

- **Director of the MIT Plasma Science and Fusion Center (PSFC)** since May 2024 — **MIT's largest laboratory**.
- **Herman Feshbach (1942) Professor of Physics**.
- In **January 2025** received the **Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE)** from President Biden.
- Co-editor of the _Journal of Plasma Physics_ since January 2025.

**Active projects:**

1. **SPARC** — collaboration between PSFC and **Commonwealth Fusion Systems** (PSFC spin-out startup). Fusion reactor **planned to ignite in 2026** and produce a **burning plasma** that generates more energy than it consumes. Loureiro described it as _"a watershed moment."_ Key technology: **high-temperature superconducting magnets developed at PSFC**.
2. **Magnetic reconnection** — his fundamental research line. His most recent works (in publication at the time of his death) modeled how reconnection is affected by:
   - Feedback across different scales.
   - Instabilities resulting from different drift velocities between electrons and ions in plasma.
   - Radiative cooling of plasma current sheets.
3. **Connections with inertial confinement fusion** — including experiments at **Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory** (where fusion ignition was achieved in 2022).
4. **Stellarator research** — second expansion line of PSFC under his direction.
5. **Quantum computing applied to plasma physics** — an emerging area in which Loureiro was recognized as a leading authority.
6. **Plasma astrophysics** — connection with MIT Kavli Institute for Astrophysics and Space Research; applications to solar flares and other phenomena.

## 2.12 Jason R. Thomas — died 2025/2026

**Work status at the time of his disappearance (December 2025):**

- **Associate Director of Chemical Biology at Novartis Institutes for BioMedical Research (NIBR)**, Cambridge, Massachusetts.
- More than **4,634 citations** accumulated in Google Scholar.

**Specific research lines:**

1. **Chemoproteomics** — particularly in the Developmental and Molecular Pathways (DMP) group at NIBR.
2. **Photoaffinity Labeling-Based Chemoproteomics** for **target deconvolution** of small molecule drug candidates. His 2017 paper (Thomas, Brittain, Lipps, Llamas, Jain, Schirle) remains the methodological reference.
3. **Targeted Protein Degradation (PROTACs)** — covalent degraders in cancers such as breast, ovarian, prostate.
4. **Studies on Nimbolide** (triterpenoid from the neem tree) — discovery of its anticancer mechanism through reactions with novel functional cysteines in E3 ligase proteins.
5. Development of **second-generation photoactivatable affinity probes (acyl silane)** for cellular chemoproteomic profiling (published 2025).

Was in a **very productive stage of his career**, with recent publications in top-tier journals (_Nature Communications_, _ACS Chemical Biology_, _Angewandte Chemie_).

## 2.13 Carl Grillmair — died 2026

**Work status at the time of his death (February 2026):**

Grillmair had **three simultaneous active fronts**.

**Institutional projects:**

1. **NEOWISE Science Data Center** — quality assurance scientist and pipeline operator on the NEOWISE mission (NASA), which hunted potentially hazardous asteroids and comets threatening Earth.
2. **NEO Surveyor** (next NASA mission) — **instrument characterization specialist**. This mission will be the successor to NEOWISE, dedicated to planetary defense.

**Collaborative projects:**

3. **Moona LISA (Lunar Impact Spectrometer Assembly)** — the last project of his life. Initiated with his former University of Calgary colleague, **Stephen Achal** (CEO of **4pi Lab** in Calgary).
   - Concept: **a spacecraft in low lunar orbit planned for 2028 or 2029** that would perform **spectroscopy of light emitted by micrometeorites impacting the lunar surface**.
   - Would allow characterization of the **near-space environment**.
   - Was testing **new instrumentation at the Hale Telescope at Palomar Observatory** during an upcoming lunar eclipse.
   - After his death, Achal completed the observation scheduled for the following Monday/Tuesday at Palomar as a tribute to Grillmair.

**Ongoing scientific research:**

4. Studies of **stellar streams** and galactic structure.
5. **Exoplanet spectroscopy** — continuation of his line inaugurated in 2007 with HD 189733b.
6. Three papers in preparation at the time of his death (from a total of **147 peer-reviewed** in his career).

## 2.14 William Neil McCasland — disappeared 2026

**Work status at the time of his disappearance (February 2026):**

- Retired from the Air Force since October 2013 (more than 12 years before his disappearance).
- **Director of Technology at Applied Technology Associates (ATA)**, Albuquerque subsidiary of **BlueHalo**.

### About BlueHalo and ATA

**BlueHalo** was formed in October 2020 through the combination of three companies: **AEgis Technologies, Applied Technology Associates, and Brilligent Solutions** — all portfolio companies of **Arlington Capital Partners**.

ATA, founded in 1975, had been a leader in:

- **Precision optical sensors.**
- **Laser communications.**
- **Electro-optical systems.**
- More than **100 SBIR programs** completed or underway.

### Active Business Areas at BlueHalo (where McCasland worked)

1. **Space Superiority and Directed Energy** — directed energy weapons (anti-drone lasers).
2. **Missile Defense and C4ISR** — missile defense systems and intelligence.
3. **Cyber and Intelligence**.
4. **Laser communications and electro-optics**.

### Relevant Contracts in the 2024–2026 Period

- **May 2024:** BlueHalo receives a contract of **US$95.4 million** from the U.S. Army Space and Missile Defense Command for the **LARDO program (Laser Technology Research Development and Optimization)** — development of anti-drone laser weapons.
- **August 2024:** ATA wins contract for the **ITATS** (Integrated Targeting Acquisition System) as part of the U.S. Army's **DE-MSHORAD** initiative (integrating directed energy weapons on Stryker vehicles).
- **November 2023:** BlueHalo deploys its **Locust** laser systems for the U.S. Marine Corps.
- **April 2024:** BlueHalo signs contract for support services for the U.S. Army's **P-HEL (Palletized High Energy Laser)** system.

### Other Active Affiliations

- **Riverside Research** — member of the Board of Trustees since June 2019 (nonprofit organization chartered for scientific research in service of the U.S. government).
- **Associate Fellow of AIAA** (American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics) since 2013.
- **Senior Member of IEEE**.
- From January to October 2024 endorsed Kamala Harris in the presidential election.

### Health Status

- Reported **"mental fog"** in the months before his disappearance.
- Under medical care for **anxiety, short-term memory loss, and sleep problems**.
- Had for these reasons left several groups he had been part of.
- His wife Susan told 911 that he **"planned not to be found."**

1. **Project phase:** Not everyone was at critical moments. Hicks, Chavez, and Sullivan were retired or post-active. Maiwald, Reza, Loureiro, Thomas, Grillmair, and McCasland were in fully productive phases. LeBlanc was in the forced closure of his program.

   - **Planetary defense:** Hicks, Grillmair (NEO Surveyor, NEOWISE).
   - **Advanced propulsion:** LeBlanc (nuclear), Reza (materials), Eskridge (anti-gravity), Li (gravitomagnetism).
   - **Nuclear fusion:** Loureiro (magnetic), Eskridge (Direct Fusion Drive, talks).
   - **Directed energy weapons:** McCasland (BlueHalo).
   - **Nuclear stockpile stewardship:** Chavez (DARHT), Garcia (KCNSC).
   - **Dual-use remote sensors:** Maiwald (SBG-VSWIR, AMR-C).
   - **Intelligence / UAP:** Sullivan.
   - **Oncological pharmaceutical research:** Thomas.
3. **Documented interpersonal connections:**
   - Reza ↔ McCasland (Mondaloy program, AFRL 2001–2004).
   - Eskridge ↔ Richard Eskridge (her father, NASA Marshall).
   - LeBlanc worked at NASA Marshall, Huntsville — the same city as the Eskridges and Ning Li.
   - Chavez and Casias at LANL.
   - Garcia (KCNSC) works with Sandia and LANL.
   - Maiwald, Hicks, Grillmair at JPL/IPAC, all in the Pasadena area.
