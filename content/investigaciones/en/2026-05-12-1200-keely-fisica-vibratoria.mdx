# Keely and Sympathetic Vibratory Physics

## John Ernst Worrell Keely (1837–1898)

Keely was an inventor from Philadelphia who claimed to have discovered a new motive force based on "vibratory sympathy" and "interatomic ether." He built devices — including the Musical Dynasphere — that witnesses reported produced motion, levitation, and water dissociation using sound and resonance.

He is a deeply controversial figure. His defenders (Dale Pond, Clara Bloomfield-Moore) consider him a genius ahead of his time. His critics consider him a fraud who never delivered reproducible results. His original writings — thousands of pages — disappeared after his death and have never been recovered.

---

## The 40 laws

Keely formulated 40 laws describing a universe where everything is vibration. The central concepts:

- **Atomoles:** fundamental particles of the ether, in perpetual vibration, the basis of all matter
- **Atomic tone:** each element vibrates at a specific frequency — its "mass chord"
- **Vibratory sympathy:** two bodies vibrating in harmony attract; in discord they repel
- **Neutral center:** a point of convergence of opposing forces where movement emerges
- **Scale of 105 octaves:** all forces (sound, heat, light, gravity, electricity) are the same universal energy at different frequencies

The laws were rescued and disseminated by Dale Pond, who spent decades collecting fragments of Keely's work and building the "Atlin" replica of the Musical Dynasphere.

---

## The Musical Dynasphere

The central device: a hollow sphere 12 inches in diameter with an inner ring of 7 resonator tubes (graduated in the diatonic scale), 18 strings tensioned inside, and a neutral center where the axes converge.

Keely claimed the Dynasphere did not create energy but organized it — tuning into the universal etheric flow through harmonic resonance. The sphere was the most natural form for establishing pure concordance between multiple resonant media.

Dale Pond achieved a partial replication ("Atlin") that did not rotate consistently but did produce a measurable energy field and, according to reports, health effects in nearby people.

## Controversy and current status

Keely never documented his experiments with enough detail to be replicable. His machines disappeared. Pond's replica did not achieve consistent operation. No functional device based on his plans exists today.

What remains is a conceptual framework that anticipated some intuitions of modern physics (matter as vibration, vacuum energy, resonance as an interaction mechanism) but without the mathematical formalization or experimental verification that would make it accepted science.
