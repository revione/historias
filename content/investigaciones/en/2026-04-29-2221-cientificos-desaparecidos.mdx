# Case Summary

Since 2022, at least **eleven scientists and employees** linked to US nuclear, space, and defense research have **died or gone missing**. In April 2026, the FBI, the Department of Energy, and the House Oversight Committee launched a **federal review** to determine whether a connection exists between the cases.

> ⚠️ **Important:** To date, **no authority has confirmed links** between the deaths and disappearances. Several cases have documented explanations (unrelated homicide, pre-existing medical conditions, suicide). The graphic circulating on social media presents **speculative connections** not officially confirmed.

## 👥 People

### Frank Maiwald — Deceased

- **Age:** 61
- **Role:** Senior researcher at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), 25 years of service.
- **Work:** Highly specialized engineer; in 2023 led a project to detect signs of life on other worlds (VSWIR instrument for the Surface Biology and Geology mission).
- **Date of death:** July 4, 2024, Los Angeles.
- **Cause:** Never made public. NASA and JPL issued no comment.

### Carl Grillmair — Deceased

- **Age:** 67
- **Role:** Astronomer at IPAC (Caltech), nearly 30-year career.
- **Work:** In 2007 led the study that **first identified molecules in an exoplanet's atmosphere**; discovered dozens of stellar streams. Received the NASA Medal for Exceptional Scientific Achievement (2011).
- **Date of death:** February 16, 2026, in Llano (Antelope Valley, California).
- **Cause:** Gunshot homicide at his residence. A suspect (Freddy Snyder, 29) was arrested who, according to Los Angeles County authorities, **did not know the victim**.

### Michael David Hicks — Deceased

- **Age:** 59
- **Role:** Research scientist at NASA JPL (1998–2022).
- **Work:** Physicist specializing in comet and asteroid properties; participated in the **DART** (asteroid deflection), NEAT, Dawn, and Deep Space 1 missions.
- **Date of death:** July 30, 2023.
- **Cause:** Not publicly disclosed. His daughter told CNN he had **pre-existing medical conditions**.

### Melissa Casias — Missing

- **Age:** 53
- **Role:** Administrative assistant at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) with security clearance.
- **Missing since:** June 26, 2025, Taos County, New Mexico.
- **Circumstances:** Last seen walking along a road. Left at home her car, purse, keys, and two phones — **both factory-reset**. The family rejects the voluntary departure hypothesis. Police do not suspect foul play.

### Anthony "Tony" Chavez — Missing

- **Age:** 78
- **Role:** Retired employee of Los Alamos National Laboratory; worked as a foreman supervising construction.
- **Missing since:** Approximately May 4, 2025, left his home in Los Alamos, New Mexico on foot.
- **Circumstances:** Extensive searches yielded no results. No signs of foul play according to a detective cited by CNN.

### Monica Jacinto Reza — Missing

- **Age:** 60
- **Role:** Director of the Materials Processing Group at NASA JPL; previously at Aerojet Rocketdyne.
- **Work:** Co-inventor of **Mondaloy**, a patented nickel superalloy for rocket engines (used in programs such as New Glenn and Starship).
- **Missing since:** June 22, 2025, while hiking near Mount Waterman in the Angeles National Forest.
- **Circumstances:** Was with a companion about 9 meters away; waved to them, and when the companion turned back around she was gone. The case was transferred to the Homicide Missing Persons Unit.

### William Neil McCasland — Missing

- **Age:** 68
- **Role:** Retired Major General of the Air Force; former commander of the **Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)** at Wright-Patterson AFB (2011–2013), managed ~$2.2 billion in scientific programs. Currently director of technology at Applied Technology Associates (subsidiary of BlueHalo).
- **Missing since:** February 27, 2026, left his home in Albuquerque, New Mexico on foot.
- **Circumstances:** Left behind his phone, glasses, and devices. Took his wallet, a .38 revolver, and a red backpack. Reported "mental fog" before disappearing. His wife told 911 that he "planned not to be found." A Silver Alert was issued. The FBI is participating in the search.

## 🏛️ Institutions Involved

### NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)

NASA's R&D center in Pasadena, California, managed by Caltech. Linked to **four** of the Los Angeles County cases: Hicks, Maiwald, Reza, and (by collaboration) Grillmair.

### Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL)

Department of Energy national laboratory in New Mexico, the country's primary **nuclear research** center. Casias and Chavez were connected to this facility.

### AFRL — Wright-Patterson Air Force Base (Ohio)

Main headquarters of the Air Force Research Laboratory. Research in advanced weapons, directed energy, aerospace materials, and satellites. Historically associated with UFO theories (Roswell rumors), though no official evidence exists. McCasland was its commander.

### AFRL — Kirtland Air Force Base (New Mexico)

Another AFRL facility, focused on space vehicles and directed weapons. **Collaborates with LANL** in research. McCasland previously commanded the Phillips Research Site there.

## 📊 Official Status of the Investigation

- **FBI:** Leading the review effort, in coordination with the Department of Energy and Department of Defense.
- **White House:** President Trump described the matter as "quite serious," though said he expected it to be "coincidence."
- **House Oversight Committee (Republican):** Requested briefings from the FBI, DOE, DoD, and NASA.
- **NASA:** Stated that "nothing related to NASA indicates a threat to national security."
- **Independent experts** (CSIS, Nuclear Threat Initiative): See no obvious pattern; the cases are spread across years, locations, and projects with no common project.

## 🔍 Conclusion

The original infographic mixes **verifiable facts** (the people existed or exist, the workplaces are real, the deaths and disappearances occurred) with **speculative connections** that have not been validated by any official investigation. Serious journalism — CNN, CBS, Newsweek, Snopes, Boston Globe — emphasizes that as of April 2026, **no public evidence of conspiracy exists**, although the scale of the cluster justifies the ongoing federal review.
