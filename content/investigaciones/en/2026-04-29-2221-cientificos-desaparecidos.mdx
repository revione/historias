# Case Summary

Since 2022, at least **eleven scientists and employees** connected to U.S. nuclear, space, and defense research have **died or gone missing**. In April 2026, the FBI, the Department of Energy, and the House Oversight Committee launched a **federal review** to determine whether the cases are connected.

> ⚠️ **Important:** To date, **no authority has confirmed any links** between the deaths and disappearances. Several cases have documented explanations (unrelated homicide, pre-existing medical conditions, suicide). The graphic circulating on social media presents **speculative connections**, not officially confirmed ones.

## 👥 People

### Frank Maiwald — Deceased

- **Age:** 61
- **Position:** Principal Investigator at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), with 25 years of service.
- **Work:** Highly specialized engineer; in 2023 he was leading a project for the detection of life signatures on other worlds (VSWIR instrument for the Surface Biology and Geology mission).
- **Date:** July 4, 2024, in Los Angeles.
- **Cause:** Never made public. NASA and JPL issued no comment.

**Access relevance:** In 2023 he was leading development of the VSWIR (Visible Shortwave Infrared) instrument for the *Surface Biology and Geology* mission, designed to remotely identify molecular signatures of life on planetary surfaces. His 25 years at JPL gave him deep knowledge of mission architectures, data pipelines, and active detection methodologies. The institutional silence of NASA and JPL regarding the cause of death contrasts with their usual communication practices in cases involving high-profile staff.

### Carl Grillmair — Deceased

- **Age:** 67
- **Position:** Astronomer at IPAC (Caltech), nearly 30-year career.
- **Work:** In 2007 he led the study that **identified molecules in an exoplanet's atmosphere for the first time**; he discovered dozens of stellar streams. He received the NASA Exceptional Scientific Achievement Medal (2011).
- **Date:** February 16, 2026, in Llano (Antelope Valley, California).
- **Cause:** Firearm homicide at his home. A suspect was arrested (Freddy Snyder, 29) who **did not know the victim**, according to Los Angeles County authorities.

**Access relevance:** In 2007 he led the first study to confirm molecules (water vapor) in an exoplanet's atmosphere via transit spectroscopy, establishing the foundational methodology of the field. His stellar stream detection technique maps the galaxy's gravitational history using debris from destroyed globular clusters. The forensic details are atypical: rural property, perpetrator with no connection to the victim, no apparent documented motive.

### Michael David Hicks — Deceased

- **Age:** 59
- **Position:** Research Scientist at NASA JPL (1998–2022).
- **Work:** Physicist specializing in the properties of comets and asteroids; participated in the **DART** (asteroid deflection), NEAT, Dawn, and Deep Space 1 missions.
- **Date:** July 30, 2023.
- **Cause:** Not publicly disclosed. His daughter told CNN he had **pre-existing medical conditions**.

**Access relevance:** He was a mission physicist for DART (Double Asteroid Redirection Test), the first successful experiment in modifying an asteroid's orbit (2022). His knowledge encompassed impactor kinematics, models of asteroid composition, and momentum-transfer calculations. The same equations that describe how to deflect an asteroid also describe how to predict and exploit its natural trajectory. He also participated in Deep Space 1, a pioneer in ion propulsion, and in NEAT, a catalog of near-Earth objects.

### Melissa Casias — Missing

- **Age:** 53
- **Position:** Administrative assistant at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) with a security clearance.
- **Date:** June 26, 2025, in Taos County, New Mexico.
- **Circumstances:** Last seen walking along a road. She left her car, purse, keys, and two phones at home — **both factory-reset**. The family rejects the hypothesis of a voluntary departure. Police do not suspect foul play.

**Access relevance:** An administrative assistant with a security clearance at a national nuclear laboratory has access to personnel management systems, schedules, logistical routes, physical access records, and internal documentation flows. The factory reset of *two* phones before disappearing is an atypical forensic detail: the deliberate elimination of digital evidence is not characteristic of a spontaneous voluntary departure. The vast majority of voluntary disappearances do not involve the methodical destruction of digital traces.

### Anthony "Tony" Chavez — Missing

- **Age:** 78
- **Position:** Retired employee of Los Alamos National Laboratory; worked as a foreman supervising construction.
- **Date:** Approximately May 4, 2025, left his home in Los Alamos, New Mexico, on foot.
- **Circumstances:** Exhaustive searches yielded no results. No indication of foul play according to a detective cited by CNN.

**Access relevance:** Chavez supervised the physical construction of DARHT (Dual Axis Radiographic Hydrotest Facility) at LANL, the only facility in the world capable of taking high-speed X-ray images from two perpendicular axes simultaneously during the implosion of a nuclear weapon. DARHT is the central instrument of the *Stockpile Stewardship Program*, which certifies weapons designs without actual nuclear testing. A construction foreman who oversaw its building knows the shielding configurations, structural blueprints, access routes, and mechanical details that appear in no public document.

### Monica Jacinto Reza — Missing

- **Age:** 60
- **Position:** Director of the Materials Processing Group at NASA JPL; previously at Aerojet Rocketdyne.
- **Work:** Co-inventor of **Mondaloy**, a patented nickel superalloy for rocket engines (used in programs such as New Glenn and Starship).
- **Date:** June 22, 2025, while hiking near Mount Waterman, Angeles National Forest.
- **Circumstances:** She was with a companion about 9 meters away; she waved, and when he turned around she was gone. The case was transferred to the Homicide Missing Persons Unit.

**Access relevance:** Reza co-invented Mondaloy, a nickel superalloy developed specifically for the extreme temperature and pressure conditions of liquid-fuel rocket engines. Mondaloy is integrated into engine components of New Glenn (Blue Origin) and Starship (SpaceX). As Director of the Materials Processing Group at JPL, she had cross-cutting visibility into materials science for both civilian and defense programs. Knowledge of the metallurgical performance limits of American rocket engines carries direct strategic value.

### William Neil McCasland — Missing

- **Age:** 68
- **Position:** Retired Major General in the Air Force; former commander of the **Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)** at Wright-Patterson AFB (2011–2013), managing ~$2.2 billion in scientific programs. Currently Director of Technology at Applied Technology Associates (a BlueHalo subsidiary).
- **Date:** February 27, 2026, left his home in Albuquerque, New Mexico, on foot.
- **Circumstances:** Left behind his phone, glasses, and devices. Took his wallet, a .38 revolver, and a red backpack. Reported "mental fog" before disappearing. His wife told 911 he "planned not to be found." A Silver Alert was issued. The FBI is participating in the search.

**Access relevance:** As AFRL commander, he oversaw a budget of ~$2.2 billion annually across directed-energy weapons, hypersonics, space vehicle propulsion, advanced materials, and electronic warfare programs. He also previously commanded the Phillips Research Site at Kirtland AFB, which operates in close collaboration with LANL. His current position at Applied Technology Associates (a BlueHalo subsidiary) represents the bridge between classified defense R&D knowledge and its application in the private sector. The objects he left behind (phone, glasses) versus what he took (wallet, revolver, backpack) suggest a planned departure, not a spontaneous cognitive episode.

## 🏛️ Entities Involved

### NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)

NASA's R&D center in Pasadena, California, managed by Caltech. Linked to **four** of the Los Angeles County cases: Hicks, Maiwald, Reza, and (through collaboration) Grillmair.

### Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL)

Department of Energy national laboratory in New Mexico, the country's primary center for **nuclear research**. Casias and Chavez were both linked to this facility.

### AFRL — Wright-Patterson Air Force Base (Ohio)

Main headquarters of the Air Force Research Laboratory. Research in advanced weapons, directed energy, aerospace materials, and satellites. Historically associated with UFO theories (Roswell rumors), though there is no official evidence. McCasland was its commander.

### AFRL — Kirtland Air Force Base (New Mexico)

Another AFRL installation, focused on space vehicles and directed-energy weapons. **Collaborates with LANL** on research. McCasland previously commanded the Phillips Research Site there.

## 📊 Official Investigation Status

- **FBI:** Leading the review effort, in coordination with the Department of Energy and the Department of Defense.
- **White House:** President Trump described the matter as "pretty serious," though he said he hoped it was "coincidence."
- **House Oversight Committee (Republican):** Requested briefings from the FBI, DOE, DoD, and NASA.
- **NASA:** Stated that "nothing related to NASA indicates a threat to national security."
- **Independent experts** (CSIS, Nuclear Threat Initiative): See no clear pattern; the cases are scattered across years, locations, and projects with no common project.

The source infographic mixes **verifiable facts** (the people existed or still exist, the workplaces are real, the deaths and disappearances occurred) with **speculative connections** that have not been validated by any official investigation.
