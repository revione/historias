# Dan A. Davidson

## Shape Power and Aether Physics

Dan A. Davidson is the author of _Shape Power: A Treatise on How Form Converts Universal Aether Into Electromagnetic And Gravitic Forces_ (1997), the most comprehensive treatise on how geometric shapes manipulate aetheric flow. His work bridges 19th-century fringe physics (Keely) with modern zero-point energy models and quantum electrodynamics.

---

## Main work

### _Shape Power_ (1997)

The book develops a unified theory where the aether (zero-point energy) is the fundamental substrate of all reality, and geometric shapes — from a drawn point to a pyramid — interact with it directly.

Key chapters:

1. **Aetheric physics of Shape Power** — the point, line, triangle, tetrahedron, 12-point tesseract, circle, spiral. How each shape channels aetheric flow differently.
2. **History** — Keely, Wilhelm Reich (orgone), Viktor Schauberger (implosion). Davidson traces a direct line between these researchers.
3. **Earth energies** — ley lines, energy grids, connections to feng-shui and chi.
4. **Pyramid energy** — experiments with copper pyramids, electrostatic generation, magnetic orientation.
5. **Sacred geometry** — Platonic solids, golden ratio, and their relation to aetheric flow.
6. **Crystals** — crystalline structures as containers of aetheric energy.
7. **Experimental research** — the dielectric gravity detector, the "do nothing coil," Joe Parr's gravity wheels.
8. **Nuclear structure** — the toroidal electron as the basis of Shape Power.
9. **Egypt expedition** (1997) — magnetic measurements at the Great Pyramid, acoustic analysis of the King's Chamber.

## The toroidal electron

Chapter 8 of _Shape Power_ presents the electron as a toroidal aether vortex. Davidson describes:

- Electric charge as a concentration difference of aether between the vortex interior and its surroundings
- The proton as another toroid of different radius and thickness
- Quarks as nested toroids within the proton
- Magnetism as aligned aether flow
- Gravity as a second-order gradient of the aether

This model is the direct precursor to the 2025 toroidal electromagnetic electron paper (arXiv 2510.22384). Davidson anticipated the toroidal electron structure decades earlier, though in aetheric language rather than Maxwell's equations.

## Documented experiments

### Wooden sticks (basic Shape Power)

Davidson drove 9 chopsticks into a styrofoam ball so they intersected at the center. He measured an electrostatic field of 250 V/m and a magnetic field of 1 milligauss at 2.5 cm. Each individual stick measured only 40 V/m. The intersection multiplied the effect.

### Pyramids with convergent lines

He built fiberglass and paper pyramids with engraved lines converging at the vertices. He measured 310 gammas of magnetic field at the base. The pyramid without marked lines measured zero. The direction of the stroke matters — aether follows the direction of tool movement.

### Joe Parr gravity wheels

Davidson documented and partially duplicated Joe Parr's experiments with rotating pyramids. Parr observed that a rotating pyramid generated a measurable "energy bubble" and that objects inside that bubble lost weight. Davidson confirmed similar effects and documented the critical parameters.

### Dielectric gravity detector

He invented a detector based on dielectrics that measured "gravity pressure" (aetheric flow) directly, with no moving parts. He used this detector to map fields around geometric shapes.

## Working method

Davidson combined physical instrumentation with trained clairvoyants. He subjected clairvoyants to validation tests (identifying magnetic fields, flow directions, energy colors) and only used those who passed consistently. He then cross-checked their observations with electronic measurements to verify both routes agreed.

This approach — using the human eye as an aetheric detection instrument — is one reason his work is not accepted in academia. But it is also the reason he could document phenomena that conventional instrumentation does not detect.

## Legacy

Davidson is a transitional figure. His work looks backward (Keely, Reich, the esoteric tradition) and forward (zero-point energy, quantum electrodynamics, toroidal electron models). His book is the most systematic attempt to build a unified theory of Shape Power — from the drawn point to the Great Pyramid — based on a single principle: form concentrates, directs, or amplifies aether flow.

The unified flow model (O, Φ, O₀) presented in this same investigations section is a later development that reinterprets Davidson's observations in terms of field coherence and measurable order parameters. Davidson measured the effects; the unified model proposes the mechanism.
