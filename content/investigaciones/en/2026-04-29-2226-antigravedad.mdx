# 1. Definitions and Conceptual Framework

## 1.1 Categories

**Gravity:** Fundamental attractive force between masses. Coupling constant ~10⁻³⁹ times weaker than the strong force. Infinite range.

**Anti-gravity** (strict sense): An equivalent repulsive force that cancels or reverses gravitational attraction. Under classical general relativity, it requires **negative mass or energy** (not observed in nature except in small quantum effects like the Casimir effect).

**Gravitational modification** (broad sense): Any local modulation of the gravitational field — attenuation (shielding), amplification, redirection, generation of gravitomagnetic fields. **Does not require violating general relativity**, depends on what is permitted within its weak-field regime.

**Reactionless/propellantless propulsion:** Broader category that includes anti-gravity but also proposals like the EM Drive and Mach Effect Thruster that do not necessarily appeal to gravity.

## 1.2 The GEM Framework (Gravito-Electro-Magnetism)

General relativity, in the weak-field limit, predicts equations formally analogous to Maxwell's electromagnetic equations:

- **Gravitoelectric field** (analogous to the electric field) — the familiar Newtonian attraction.
- **Gravitomagnetic field** (analogous to the magnetic field) — generated by moving masses. Predicted by GR but **virtually negligible** for terrestrial masses.

The **frame-dragging (Lense-Thirring) effect** — the "twisting" of spacetime around rotating masses — was experimentally confirmed by:

- **Gravity Probe B (NASA-Stanford, launched 2004, results 2011)** — confirmed the effect to ~10% precision.
- **LAGEOS / LARES (Ciufolini, 2004)** — confirmed to ~10%.

The open question is: **Can superconductors produce gravitomagnetic fields orders of magnitude larger than those predicted by classical GR?**

# 2. The Pre-Superconductor Era (1920–1970s)

## 2.1 Thomas Townsend Brown and the Biefeld-Brown Effect (1920s)

- Brown observed that **high-voltage capacitors with asymmetric dielectrics** experience a net force when charged.
- Coined the term **"electrogravitics."**
- Patent of 1960 (US 2,949,550 and others).
- **Current status:** Most likely due to **ion wind** (corona currents in air), although vacuum tests supposedly partially rule this out. No accepted theory connects it to gravity.

## 2.2 Nikola Tesla and the "Dynamic Theory of Gravity" (1928)

- Patent 1,655,144 — "anti-electromagnetic" propulsion system.
- Tesla rejected Einstein's spacetime curvature and proposed that matter consisted of "ether vortices."
- **Current status:** Pseudoscientific from a modern perspective, but recurrently cited by enthusiasts.

## 2.3 Roger Babson's Gravity Research Foundation (1948)

- Foundation created by economist Roger Babson, motivated by the drowning death of his sister (which he attributed "to gravity").
- Awards **annual prizes for essays on gravity**.
- Prize winners include: **Stephen Hawking** and **5 future Nobel laureates**.
- **Current status:** Active. The prize retains academic relevance.

## 2.4 Alleged Wright-Patterson / Lockheed Programs (1950s)

- The **Aeronautical Research Laboratory** supposedly conducted research at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base (1956).
- The **Research Institute for Advanced Study** conducted research that became **Lockheed Skunk Works** in later years.
- Limited public documentation.

## 2.5 Curiosities of the Era

- **Searl Generator (1946):** John Searl claimed to have invented a combined anti-gravity and free energy device. No replication.
- **Dean Drive (1950s-60s):** Norman Dean proposed a mechanical "reactionless drive." The effect is attributed today to friction.
- **Wallace Machine (1968):** Henry William Wallace patented devices based on rotating bronze discs to align "nuclear spin" and generate a "kinemassic field." No support.
- **Laithwaite Gyroscopes (1974):** Eric Laithwaite (inventor of the Maglev) publicly demonstrated that rotating gyroscopes appeared to lose weight. Today explained correctly by Newton's 2nd law.

## 2.6 Bryce DeWitt and the Serious Academic Line

In 1953, in an essay for the Gravity Research Foundation, physicist Bryce DeWitt wrote:

> _"Before anyone has the audacity to formulate even the most rudimentary plan of attack on the problem of harnessing the force of gravitation, he must understand the nature of his adversary. I take as axiomatic that the phenomenon of gravitation is poorly understood even by the best minds, and the last word on it is very far from having been said."_

This phrase is the **epistemic cornerstone** of the field. DeWitt was one of the giants of twentieth-century quantum gravity.

# 3. The Institutional Blockade: Mansfield Amendment (1973)

## 3.1 What It Was

**Section 203 of Public Law 91-121** (1969, known as the **Mansfield Amendment** after its author Senator Mike Mansfield, Democrat of Montana) restricted defense research appropriations so that they could **only be granted to projects with direct military application**.

The current codified form is in **10 U.S. Code § 4001**:

> _"Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense may not be used to fund any research project or study unless the project or study has, in the opinion of the Secretary, a potential relationship to a military function or operation."_

The 1969 version was stricter ("direct military application"); the 1973 amendment maintained the principle.

## 3.2 The Pre-Mansfield Era (1945–1969)

For 24 years, the DoD was the **primary sponsor of unrestricted basic research** at American universities, particularly in physics, mathematics, materials science, and computer science. The key agencies were:

- **Advanced Research Projects Agency (ARPA, founded 1958)** — predecessor to DARPA.
- **Office of Naval Research (ONR)**.
- **Air Force Office of Scientific Research (AFOSR)**.
- **Army Research Office**.

Program managers (Jack Ruina, Eberhardt Rechtin, Stephen Lukasik) could **fund brilliant iconoclasts for decades based on intuition**. Without peer-review panels, without consensus requirements, without short-term deliverables.

## 3.3 The "Golden Age of General Relativity" (1955–1973/75)

Science historians (Jean Eisenstaedt, Clifford Will, Kip Thorne, Jürgen Renn) **explicitly date** the golden age of general relativity between 1955 and 1973/75 — ending exactly on the Mansfield timeline.

In that period were produced:

- New exact solutions to Einstein's equations.
- Global methods (Penrose, Hawking).
- Astrophysical applications (black holes, gravitational waves).
- Dozens of active university groups in gravity, field theory, and relativity — **70-90% funded by the DoD**.

## 3.4 The Post-Mansfield Era (1973–present)

The documented effects:

- **"Brain drain"** — young scientists left universities for startups and private labs (Xerox PARC, etc.).
- **The gravity field shifted from explosive growth to near-dormancy** outside a few astrophysical niches.
- The National Science Foundation (NSF) **never compensated the difference** as expected.
- The new rules (NSF): peer review, solid preliminary data, punishment for paradigm deviation, obsession with citations.
- Exotic gravitational projects became trapped between two worlds: too speculative for NSF, too basic for post-Mansfield DoD.

## 3.5 Amy Eskridge's Interpretation

Amy Eskridge (Institute for Exotic Science, 2018) identified the Mansfield Amendment as **the milestone that displaced anti-gravity research out of the open academic sphere**. Her thesis: any serious research post-1973 has had to occur in:

1. **Private sector** with NDAs (non-disclosure agreements).
2. **Black budgets** — special access programs without public traceability.
3. **Modest and isolated civil programs** (BPP, NIAC).

## 3.6 Positive Collateral Consequence

Ironically, the "brain drain" caused by Mansfield is **credited with having propelled the personal computer industry** — scientists who left universities founded Xerox PARC, which led to Apple, Microsoft, etc. Sometimes the institutional effects of cutting basic funding have unexpected consequences.

# 4. The Modern Era: Superconductors and Gravity (1989–present)

## 4.1 The Foundational Hypothesis of Ning Li and Douglas Torr (UAH, 1989–1994)

### The Central Insight

The **crystal lattice ions** (not the Cooper pairs) are the effective carriers of quantized angular momentum in a superconductor. If those ions rotate coherently, they generate a gravitomagnetic field analogous to the magnetic field of moving charges.

### The Four Fundamental Papers

1. **Li, N. & Torr, D.G. (1991)**, _Physical Review D_ 43(2): 457–459 — "Effects of a gravitomagnetic field on pure superconductors."
2. **Li, N. & Torr, D.G. (1992)**, _Physical Review B_ 46(9): 5489–5495 — "Gravitational effects on the magnetic attenuation of superconductors."
3. **Torr, D.G. & Li, N. (1993)**, _Foundations of Physics Letters_ 6(4): 371–383 — "Gravito-electric coupling via superconductivity."
4. **Li, N., Torr, D.G. & Karwacki, F.A. (1994)**, UAH preprint — "Lattice ion rotations and the dipole gravitomagnetic field of superconductors."

### Key Prediction

If a large number of ions can be aligned — for example, by trapping them in a lattice inside a **rotating high-temperature superconductor (HTSC) disc** — the collective effect could produce a detectable gravitomagnetic field. Under a time-dependent magnetic vector potential, a gravitoelectric field could additionally be induced.

### The Formal Academic Criticism

**Edward G. Harris (1999)**, _Foundations of Physics Letters_ 12: 201–208 — "Comments on 'Gravitoelectric-Electric Coupling via Superconductivity' by Douglas G. Torr and Ning Li." Argued that the fields calculated by Torr and Li were **too large by many orders of magnitude** due to unrealistic assumptions.

## 4.2 The NASA Marshall Phase (1996–2002)

NASA Marshall Space Flight Center, together with UAH, attempted to replicate the effect. The project was led by:

- **Ron Koczor** (project manager at MSFC).
- **David Noever** (theoretical physicist).
- **Tony Robertson, Whitt Brantley** (experimental support).

### Resulting Publications

- **Li, N., Noever, D., Robertson, T., Koczor, R. & Brantley, W. (1997)**, "Static Test for a Gravitational Force Coupled to Type II YBCO Superconductors", _Physica C_ 281: 260–267. **Result:** the maximum contribution to a change in the gravity of a static superconductor in a constant magnetic field was measured as **less than 2 parts in 10⁸ of normal gravitational acceleration**. The paper explicitly leaves open "the rotating version" of the experiment for later works.
- **Noever, D. & Koczor, R.**, "Radio-frequency Illuminated Superconductive Disks: Reverse Josephson Effects and Implications for Precise Measuring of Proposed Gravity Effects," presented at the 9th Advanced Space Propulsion Research Workshop at NASA JPL.
- **Noever, D., Koczor, R. & Robertson, R. (1998)**, "Superconductor-Mediated Modification of Gravity? AC Motor Experiments with Bulk YBCO Disks in Rotating Magnetic Fields," AIAA-98-3139.

### The Experiment That Never Completed

In 1999, several single-layer superconducting discs were manufactured in UAH-Marshall collaboration. Podkletnov was hired as a consultant and a **bilayer disc** was constructed. **The complete experiment could never be finished** due to resource constraints.

## 4.3 Ning Li's Post-Academic Career

### 1999: Departure from UAH

Ning Li left the University of Alabama in Huntsville in 1999 to found **AC Gravity LLC**. The departure was preceded by an **"academic turf war"** reported by the Huntsville Times — she was frustrated with the pace of research at Marshall.

### 2001: DoD Contract

**AC Gravity LLC** received a Department of Defense contract of **US$448,970** in 2001 to continue anti-gravity research. **No results were published.**

### 2003: Last Public Appearance

In 2003, Li presented **"Measurability of AC Gravity Fields"** at the **MITRE Corporation** conference (the organization that manages multi-agency federal research). **Co-presenter: an officer from Redstone Arsenal's U.S. Army Aviation and Missile Command.** This demonstrates that **her work continued to be active and under the Army's umbrella**.

### May 2003: The Last Email

Private email to colleagues in which Li said she had observed **"11 kilowatts of output effect"** in an experiment. **It was never publicly clarified what that magnitude meant.**

### 2003–2014: Continuous Work at Redstone Arsenal

According to statements by her son George Guangyu Men to the Huntsville Business Journal (2023), **Li never left the DoD**, **never returned to China**, **never defected**. She worked at Redstone Arsenal every day until her accident in 2014. There was a visit from CCP officials in 2008 who wanted to recruit her; she declined.

### Robert Baker and the "Academic Review Committee"

Dr. Robert Baker — who was **a member of the Academic Review Committee on Gravitational Research of the U.S. Army (Redstone Arsenal) between 2001-2003** — said in a 2008 email: _"I was evaluating her work on HFGW (High Frequency Gravitational Waves) and superconductors for the U.S. Army. According to them she never presented them with a final report for the US$500,000 contract they paid her."_ (The figure US$500,000 differs from the US$448,970 in official sources — possibly refers to an additional contract or total amount with renewals.)

### The Observed Change

Her son George documented the **"visible cost"** of the transition from academia to classified work: _"When she was at the University, she loved to publish her findings."_ In the classified sector, she could no longer do so. She died in 2021 without having published her post-2003 work.

## 4.4 Eugene Podkletnov's Replication (1992)

### The Original Experiment

**E. Podkletnov & R. Nieminen (1992)**, "A Possibility of Gravitational Force Shielding by Bulk YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋ₓ Superconductor", _Physica C_ 203: 441–444.

Configuration:

- YBCO superconducting disc of ~30 cm diameter.
- Cooled to cryogenic temperature.
- Levitated over magnets by the Meissner effect.
- Set to rotate like a compact disc.
- A test mass above the disc.

Reported result: **weight loss of 0.05% to 2.1%**. Called the effect **"gravitational force shielding."**

### The 1997 Refinement

**Podkletnov, E. (1997)**, "Weak gravitational shielding properties of composite bulk YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋ₓ superconductor below 70 K under EM field", LANL cond-mat/9701074.

### The Tampere Firing

Podkletnov was **fired from the University of Tampere in 1997** after sensationalist media coverage (_Sunday Telegraph_, _New Scientist_: _"Breakthrough as scientists beat gravity,"_ September 1996).

# 5. Public Institutional Programs

## 5.1 NASA Breakthrough Propulsion Physics Program (BPP) (1996–2002)

| Attribute | Data |
| --- | --- |
| Headquarters | NASA Lewis Research Center (later Glenn Research Center), Cleveland, Ohio |
| Director | Marc G. Millis (aerospace engineer) |
| Active period | 1996–2002 (6 years) |
| Total funding | **US$1.2 million** (modest) |

### Stated Objectives

1. **Propellantless propulsion.**
2. **Hyper-fast speeds** (including, if possible, superluminal).
3. **On-board energy production methods** capable of powering such systems.

### Fields Investigated

- **Gravity-electromagnetism coupling.**
- **Energy from vacuum fluctuations** (Casimir effect, Lamb shift).
- **Warp drives and wormholes.**
- **Superluminal quantum tunneling.**

### Funding Structure

- **5 externally funded projects.**
- **2 internal tasks.**
- **1 minor grant.**

### Cancellation

In 2002, when the Advanced Space Transportation Program was reorganized, **all research with TRL (Technology Readiness Level) below 3** was eliminated. The BPP was cancelled.

### Final Production

Marc G. Millis & Eric W. Davis (eds.), **"Frontiers of Propulsion Science,"** AIAA, 2009 (739 pages) — the book documenting the program's findings.

### Civil Succession

Millis retired from NASA in February 2010 and founded the **Tau Zero Foundation** to continue research in a non-governmental form.

## 5.2 Boeing GRASP (Gravity Research for Advanced Space Propulsion) (~2002–2010s)

- Program of **Boeing Phantom Works.**
- Publicly acknowledged in 2014.
- Focused on investigating Podkletnov's gravitational shielding and "impulse beam" variants.
- **Podkletnov was reported as a collaborator** in the research.
- **Did not publish positive results** in open literature.

## 5.3 NASA NIAC (NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts)

- Currently active program.
- Funded in 2017–2018 studies on the **Mach Effect Thruster** by James Woodward.
- Functions as a partial heir to the BPP, though with smaller scale and more conservative scope.

## 5.4 Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) — "The Role of Superconductors in Gravity Research" (2010)

In 2010, the DIA published a comprehensive report titled **"The Role of Superconductors in Gravity Research,"** accessible via FOIA. This is one of the **few official recognitions of the field** by a U.S. government agency. It contains critical reviews of the work of:

- Ning Li and Douglas Torr.
- Eugene Podkletnov.
- Giovanni Modanese.
- Martin Tajmar.
- Claude Poher.
- Ron Koczor and David Noever (NASA Marshall).

# 7. The Tajmar-de Matos Line (Austria/ESA, 2003–2011)

## 7.1 Who They Are

- **Martin Tajmar** — Austrian physicist. Initially at ARC Seibersdorf Research GmbH, later TU Dresden.
- **Clovis J. de Matos** — physicist at the **European Space Agency (ESA-HQ, Paris)**.

## 7.2 The Hypothesis: the "Gravitomagnetic London Moment"

### The Insight

There is a **known anomaly**: high-precision measurements of the mass of Cooper pairs (Tate et al., 1980s) slightly deviate from quantum theory predictions.

Tajmar and de Matos conjectured that this anomaly might be explained by the appearance of **a gravitomagnetic field in the rotating superconductor** analogous to the magnetic "London moment." They called it the **"Gravitomagnetic London Moment."**

### The Theoretical Mechanism

They drew inspiration from superconductivity itself: the electromagnetic properties of superconductors are explained in quantum theory by assuming that **photons (carriers of electromagnetism) gain mass** in the medium. By analogy: if **gravitons (hypothetical carriers of gravity) gain mass** inside the superconductor, the anomalously large gravitomagnetic force could be modeled.

## 7.3 The Experiments

### 2006: Reported Detection

**Tajmar, M., Plesescu, F., Marhold, K. & de Matos, C.J. (2006)**, "Experimental Detection of the Gravitomagnetic London Moment," arXiv: gr-qc/0603033.

Configuration:

- Niobium superconducting ring.
- Rotating up to **6,500 rpm**.
- Accelerometers and laser gyroscopes near the ring.

Reported results:

- Acceleration fields induced outside the superconductor on the order of **~10⁻⁴ g**.
- **The field is proportional to the angular acceleration** of the superconductor.
- **18 orders of magnitude larger** than classical GR predictions for comparable masses.
- Also observed for **lead** (~84% of the niobium effect, consistent with their Cooper-pair/bulk density model).

### 2007: Refinement

**Tajmar et al. (2007)**, "Measurement of Gravitomagnetic and Acceleration Fields Around Rotating Superconductors," arXiv: gr-qc/0610015. Measurements with niobium within a factor of 2 of his theoretical prediction.

### 2008: The Parity Anomaly

With 4 gyroscopes mounted in 3 positions, Tajmar observed signals for **Al, Nb, and YBCO**, but **only for clockwise rotation** — a **parity violation effect** not expected by any model.

### 2009: Liquid and Superfluid Helium

Found that liquid and superfluid helium rotating with the superconductors **affected the results** — suspicion of experimental contamination.

### 2011: The Partial Retraction

**Tajmar et al. (2011)** reinterpreted their results, attributing the anomalous effects to **some type of acoustic noise**. New experiments with modified equipment measured signals ~2 orders of magnitude smaller.

## 7.4 The Canterbury Replication (2007)

University of Canterbury, New Zealand. Graham et al. measured gravitomagnetic fields generated by a rotating cryogenic lead disc. Their results:

- Noisy data.
- Saw a **dip** consistent with Tajmar's effect.
- The team erred on the side of caution and **did not claim detection** given insufficient statistical significance.
- Trend of the data: supported Tajmar.

## 7.5 Current Status

Tajmar is now **professor at TU Dresden** (Germany). Still active in exotic propulsion research. His most recent work investigates the EM Drive and other concepts. The **scientific community considers the Gravitomagnetic London Moment as not robustly replicated**, but the case is not completely closed given that Tajmar himself retracted the original magnitude.

# 8. The Modanese Line (Theory)

## 8.1 Who He Is

**Giovanni Modanese** — Italian physicist. Professor at the Free University of Bolzano (Italy). Alexander von Humboldt Fellow (residency at the Max-Planck-Institut für Physik in Munich, 1990s).

## 8.2 His Key Theoretical Contributions

### 1996: The Local Cosmological Constant

**Modanese, G. (1996)**, "Theoretical analysis of a reported weak gravitational shielding effect," _Europhysics Letters_ 35: 413–418.

**Modanese, G. (1996)**, "Role of a 'local' cosmological constant in Euclidean quantum gravity," _Physical Review D_ 54: 5002.

His thesis: the Podkletnov effect **has no explanation in standard gravity theories**, except possibly in **non-perturbative Euclidean quantum gravity**, where a **local Λ term** (local cosmological constant) might appear in the presence of a bosonic condensate (the Cooper pairs).

### 2000s: Condensate-Gravity Coupling

Modanese developed a formalism in which the supercondensate acts as an additional gravitational "source" through its vacuum expectation value (vev), modifying the local metric under specific conditions.

## 8.3 Collaboration with Podkletnov

Since 2001, Modanese has been Podkletnov's main **theoretical consultant**. His role: to give theoretical consistency to the reported experimental results.

## 8.4 Current Status

Active. Has published consistently in _EPL_, _Physical Review D_, _Foundations of Physics Letters_, and quantum gravity journals. His Wikipedia was deleted at some point (Eskridge cited this as an example of "panning" in her 2018 talk), although his academic papers remain accessible.

# 9. The Ummarino-Gallerati Line (Modern Modeling)

## 9.1 Who They Are

- **Giovanni Alberto Ummarino** — physicist at the **Politecnico di Torino**.
- **Antonio Gallerati** — frequent collaborator.

## 9.2 Their Contributions

They have developed one of the **most rigorous modern theoretical frameworks** connecting:

- The **gravito-Maxwell equations** (weak-field limit of GR).
- The **time-dependent Ginzburg-Landau (TDGL) theory** for superconductors.

### Key Papers

- **Ummarino, G.A. & Gallerati, A. (2017)**, "Superconductor in a weak static gravitational field," _European Physical Journal C_ 77: 549.
- **Ummarino, G.A. & Gallerati, A. (2021)**, "Possible Alterations of Local Gravitational Field Inside a Superconductor," _Symmetry_ 13(3): 385.

## 9.3 The Prediction

Under specific conditions of superfluid density and external magnetic fields, **Δ** (the variation of the external gravitational field inside the superconductor) **would in principle be measurable**, especially in high-Tc superconductors. The problem is that the effect manifests in extremely short time intervals.

## 9.4 Optimal Materials

Their analysis suggests that **high-temperature cuprates** (YBCO, BSCCO) and **iron superconductors** are optimal candidates to maximize the effect.

## 9.5 Current Status

Active. They form part of the **mainstream-but-open** academic consensus of the field: the idea of gravitational modification via superconductors is not absurd in principle, but the predicted effects are extremely small.

# 10. The Poher Line (France, 2000s–present)

## 10.1 Who He Is

**Claude Poher** — French physicist and engineer. Former director of **CNES (Centre National d'Études Spatiales)**, the French space agency. His son, also a physicist and engineer, has collaborated.

## 10.2 The "Universons" Theory

Poher developed a proprietary theory postulating hypothetical particles called **"universons"** that mediate gravitational and electromagnetic interactions. **Not an accepted mainstream theory.**

## 10.3 The Experiments

Modification of the Podkletnov effect:

- Instead of levitation + rotation, uses **direct electrical discharge** through a YBCO disc.
- Reported **propulsive force** resulting from the discharge.
- Tested at **NASA Marshall in 2015**.

## 10.4 Status

Poher continues working. Has attempted commercialization (without known success). In 2011, together with his son, published a study that **partially replicated Modanese's work**, but explicitly avoided labeling the force as "gravitational," calling it instead **"unknown force"** or **"propulsive phenomenon."**

# 12. The Podkletnov-Modanese Impulse Gravity Generator (2001)

## 12.1 The Conceptual Transition

Podkletnov moved from **static rotational shielding** (1992) to an entirely different phenomenon: a **pulsed force beam** generated by **high-voltage electrical discharge** through a superconductor.

## 12.2 The Original Paper

**Podkletnov, E. & Modanese, G. (2001)**, "Impulse Gravity Generator Based on Charged YBa₂Cu₃O₇₋ᵧ Superconductor with Composite Crystal Structure," arXiv: physics/0108005.

## 12.3 The Experimental Claims

- **Marx generator** discharges >5 megavolts through a superconducting emitter.
- Pulse of **60-70 nanoseconds**.
- Generates a **"force beam"** that:
  - Can **knock over objects in its trajectory**.
  - **Can make holes in brick walls**.
  - Can **deform metal plates an inch thick**.
  - **Penetrates several concrete buildings** (experiment reported at 5 km distance).
  - Reported speed: **~64 times the speed of light** (measured with two synchronized rubidium atomic clocks).
  - **Carries no electric charge.**
  - Penetrates materials **without apparent absorption or scattering**.

## 12.4 Independent Replications

### Göde Foundation (Germany)

- **Tobias Junker (2011)**, "Setup of the impulse gravity generator experiment replication," chapter in _Gravity-Superconductors Interaction: Theory and Experiment_ (Bentham).
- **Result: NULL.**
- The energy generated by a possible anomalous radiation was **9 orders of magnitude smaller** than the smallest "gravitational pulse" reported by Podkletnov.

### Poher and Schroeder

Reported **partially positive results** with their own configuration (direct discharge through a YBCO).

## 12.5 Current Status of the IGG

Critical. The Göde Foundation replication, conducted with care following the original guidelines as closely as possible, **did not find the effect**. The scientific community treats the IGG as **not replicated**, and many scientists conclude the original experiment was "never really performed."

## 12.6 Propulsion Analysis

**Robertson, G. (2003)** and others analyzed the IGG in the context of **directed space propulsion**. If the beam were real, it would be an **excellent candidate for directed space propulsion** — a spacecraft could be pushed by a beam from a ground or lunar station.

# 13. Non-Gravitational Reactionless Propulsion

## 13.1 Woodward Effect / Mach Effect (1990–present)

### The Concept

**Jim Woodward** (Cal State Fullerton, now retired) proposed a "reactionless thruster" based on the **Mach Principle**: the idea that local inertia depends on the total mass of the universe. If the inertial mass of an object can be transiently modulated, in principle thrust can be generated without propellant.

### The Device

**MEGA Drive / Mach Effect Thruster (MET)** — uses piezoelectric crystals in a PZT stack to induce inertial mass variations in an accelerometer.

### Funding

Received funding from **NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC)** in 2017–2018.

### Status

Independent replications have been **inconsistent**. Some groups detect the effect, others do not. No consensus.

## 13.2 EM Drive (Roger Shawyer, 2001)

### The Concept

A **conical microwave cavity** that, when excited with RF, supposedly produces **net thrust without propellant**. Would violate conservation of momentum if real.

### The Claims

NASA Eagleworks (Harold "Sonny" White, 2016) reported small thrust (~1 mN) in a vacuum chamber.

### The Closure

**Tajmar et al. (2018, TU Dresden)** conducted independent tests with far superior sensitivity. **Result: the "detected signals" were attributed to magnetic interaction with cables, not real thrust.** The current scientific consensus: **the EM Drive does not work.**

## 13.3 Diametric Drive (Forward & Woodward)

If blocks of negative mass existed, attaching them to positive masses would accelerate the system indefinitely without propellant. Theoretically analyzed within NASA BPP. No experimental evidence of negative mass, so it is **pure speculation**.

# 17. Conclusions on the State of the Art

## 17.1 What We Know with High Confidence

1. **Gravito-electromagnetism is real** as the weak-field limit of GR. Frame-dragging confirmed by Gravity Probe B.
2. **Superconductors produce the magnetic London moment.** This is standard physics.
3. **The Mansfield Amendment of 1973 cut open funding** for speculative gravitational research in the U.S. and displaced research to the private/classified sector.
4. **There existed a substantial NASA program (BPP, 1996–2002)** that produced peer-reviewed bibliography and an academic book (Frontiers of Propulsion Science).
5. **There existed a DoD contract to Ning Li (2001)** that kept her working at Redstone Arsenal until 2014 without public publication of results.

## 17.2 What Is in Dispute

1. **Whether rotating superconductors produce amplified gravitomagnetic effects.** Tajmar reported positive, then partially retracted. Others: inconclusive.
2. **Whether Podkletnov's gravitational shielding is real.** Negative replications; but NASA Marshall never completed the rotating version.
3. **Whether the Impulse Gravity Generator works.** Negative replication from Göde Foundation; partial replication from Poher.
4. **Whether the Mach Effect Thruster generates real thrust.** Inconsistent between groups.

## 17.3 What Appears Ruled Out

1. **The EM Drive** — refuted by Tajmar (TU Dresden, 2018).
2. **Classical mechanical reactionless drives** (Dean, Wallace) — explicable by Newtonian physics.
3. **Classical anti-gravity via negative mass** — no evidence of negative mass.

## 17.4 The Meta-Scientific Question

Three **mutually exclusive** possibilities remain:

**(A)** The effect does not exist. The robust negative replication has the final word; the positives were artifacts.

**(B)** The effect exists but is **extremely sensitive to experimental conditions**. The positives are real but so are the negatives — because the optimal conditions are difficult to reproduce. (This is the position of Modanese, Ummarino, Tajmar partially, Poher.)

**(C)** The effect exists and has been robustly replicated, but **in classified programs** that do not publish. The public negative results reflect suboptimal implementations; the public positive results attracted classification. (This is Eskridge's thesis, grounded in the Mansfield-AC Gravity-GRASP pattern.)

**There is currently no way to discriminate between (A), (B), and (C) with public data.** That is, ultimately, the honest situation of the field in April 2026.

## 17.5 Potentially Illuminating Future Directions

- **Large-scale replications with far superior sensitivity** (factor >100 over existing).
- **New superconducting materials** (advanced cuprates, high-pressure hydrides, possible ambient superconductors when available).
- **SPARC, ITER, and other fusion experiments** — can generate extreme conditions that test the theoretical models as a byproduct.
- **Precision measurements of Cooper pair mass** (the Tate anomaly) — key to validating or refuting the Tajmar/de Matos model.
- **Declassification of existing programs** — the only way to resolve possibility (C).
